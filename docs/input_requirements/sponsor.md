User Story

As a sponsor, I want a dedicated page promoting my product so that viewers can learn more about and interact with my brand.

Acceptance Criteria

- Given the event posters or main page, the sponsor’s banner should be clearly visible.
- When a user taps or hovers over the banner, then they should be taken to a dedicated page displaying information about the sponsor’s product and brand.
- The dedicated page should provide relevant product and brand information in a clear and engaging manner.

Non-Functional Requirements

1. Performance: The sponsor banner and dedicated page should load quickly, remain responsive, and provide smooth hover effects and animations.
2. Security & Privacy: Only necessary company and product information should be displayed, and sensitive or confidential company details must not be exposed.

Priority
Must Have

Requested By
Sponsor
